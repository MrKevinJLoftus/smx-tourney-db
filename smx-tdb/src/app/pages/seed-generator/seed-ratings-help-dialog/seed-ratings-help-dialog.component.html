How ratings are calculated

Suggested seedings use precomputed Glicko-2 ratings derived from match history in this database. Each player gets a rating (strength estimate), an RD (rating deviation, a confidence measure), and a count of matches used.

What matches count

  • Only strictly 1v1 matches with a recorded winner are included.
  • Matches with three or more players are skipped.

How Glicko-2 ratings are built

  • All eligible 1v1 matches are replayed in chronological order, grouped by event.
  • Each event acts as a rating period: results within that event update player ratings together.
  • Beating a stronger opponent raises your rating more; losing to a weaker opponent lowers it more.
  • Ratings are stored in the database and rebuilt after match imports.

How seeding is ordered

When you generate a seeding for your roster, players are sorted best-to-worst using this tiebreak chain:

  1. Glicko-2 rating (higher = better seed)
  2. Head-to-head record among the selected players
  3. Username (alphabetical)
  4. Provisional-last if still tied

Provisional ratings

A player is marked Provisional when they have fewer than 5 counted 1v1 matches or their RD is above 150, meaning the rating is still uncertain. Players without any rating history start at the default rating of 1500 and are also provisional.
