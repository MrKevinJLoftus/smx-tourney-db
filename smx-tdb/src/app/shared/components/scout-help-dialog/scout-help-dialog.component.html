How protects and vetos are chosen

Suggestions are based on each player's StatManiaX personal bests for the selected difficulty. Scores are fetched live when you compare and may be up to ~15 minutes behind the game.

What charts are considered

  • Only charts in the selected difficulty mode (for example, Wild).
  • Charts are filtered to the level min/max range you set, if any.
  • The comparison includes any chart where you or at least one opponent has a score on record for that mode.

How scores are compared

  • For each chart, your score is compared against the highest opponent score among the selected opponents.
  • If an opponent has no score on a chart, they are treated as 0 for that chart.
  • Delta = your score minus the best opponent score. Positive delta means you lead; negative means an opponent leads.

Protects

Charts where you have a score and your delta is positive (you beat the best opponent score).

Sorted by delta, highest first, then by your score. Up to 50 charts are shown.

Closest matchups (fallback)

If there are no protects, the same panel shows your closest matchups instead: charts where you have a score but trail or tie the best opponent (delta ≤ 0).

Sorted by delta, least negative first (smallest gap behind). Ties are labeled Even.

Vetos

Charts where an opponent's best score is higher than yours. If you have no score on a chart, your score is treated as 0 for this check.

Sorted by delta, most negative first (biggest disadvantage). Up to 50 charts are shown.
